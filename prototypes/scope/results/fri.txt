Reading: from two or three multi-bit samples 33 ns apart the edge is located to about a nanosecond,
far below the sample period but far above the TDC's 20 ps. For digital edges the TDC is the right
tool: a comparator plus a timestamp IS a one-edge FRI sampler with an ideal kernel. FRI earns its
keep where no comparator can see the event: pulses smaller than the pad's decision overdrive, edges
behind an unknown offset, or amplitude and time wanted jointly from a slow, averaged channel.
