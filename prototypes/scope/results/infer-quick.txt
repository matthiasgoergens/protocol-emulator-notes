    rise 250 ns (pass) against 350 ns (fail); actions: (instant, threshold) pairs; noise on the threshold
    from the pad (2.3 mV) and on the instant (TDC, 20 ps); one sample per released edge.
   edge within spec                   K=2 greedy   mean    2.0 samples (max    2), errors 0/100

   true rise 300 ns: decided pass 0, fail 25, mean 2.0 samples
   true rise 320 ns: decided pass 0, fail 25, mean 2.0 samples
    reconstruct instead: demo_i2c.py used 1.8 M one-bit samples for the whole waveform.

CRC consistency has no such shortcut: a CRC check needs every bit it covers (any unread bit can flip the
answer), so it is a decode question, and the chip's CRC unit on the bit path answers it at line rate.

CAUTION: action probabilities are finite-Monte-Carlo point estimates clamped to [0.001, 0.999]. The
posterior treats them as exact, so the 0.999 stop is not a calibrated 0.1 % error guarantee. The reported
trial errors measure these simulations only; zero errors in finite trials is not evidence of zero error rate.
