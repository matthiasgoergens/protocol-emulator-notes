Delegated adversarial review, 2026-09-30

The cheap subagent independently reconstructed the retained PAL RTL trace:
all 10,000 phase bytes and 40,000 quarter bits matched the 24-bit recurrence.
It identified two defects in the initial judge: PAL sign selection depended
on absolute carrier phase, and the sync gate demanded integer detected
crossings despite filtering. Both corrections are described in README.md;
the original run is retained at results-initial/ and commit 34a39ef.

Remaining concerns preserved from the incremental review:

- PAL quarter-grid still misses the predeclared 2-degree hue target.
- Ideal-carrier phase calibration shows that the TV itself has picture
  dependence on starting phase; the experiment pairs each NCO with its
  ideal waveform at the same phase.
- The 512 synthetic PAL sign checks cover a common phase rotation, not
  independent adjacent-line drift or noisy bursts.
- Paired sync error supports a relative comparison, not absolute TV lock
  robustness or a standards-compliance claim.
- Carrier-only RTL verification does not verify hue/amplitude controls,
  the resistor DAC, filter, four-phase stage connection or pads.
- Square-wave versus sinusoidal-reference error includes harmonics and
  gating as well as temporal quantisation.

This was a delegated same-family review, not a cross-model headless review.
The quantitative results were checked locally through analyse.py, including
fresh-process agreement and nominal phase-zero image parity.

A second cheap subagent checked the final README and architecture claims
against every observation group. It confirmed the ranges, pass counts and
paired sync maxima, and checked all three recorded source hashes against
the current files. It reported no additional overclaim in the final text.
