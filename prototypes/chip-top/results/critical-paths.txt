Critical paths of the hardened chip at the slow corner (nom_slow_1p08V_125C), by RTL origin.

Runs: results/harden-pe4 (/var/tmp/chip-top-harden/pe4-apart) and results/harden-pe8
(/var/tmp/chip-top-harden/pe8-apart), 20 ns clock. Netlist names are mapped back to the OCaml
call sites that built them by bin/origins.exe (Hardcaml's caller ids; an anonymous signal _N of
the Verilog is the net chip._N) and synth/paths.py; synth/sta.sh reran OpenSTA on the final
netlist and SPEF with up to 4 paths per endpoint (3,000 paths), so that paths hidden behind the
single worst start point show too. The "-noes" tables repeat it with the edge sampler's valid
register set false (its bit register, built on the same source line, remains), to show the next
tier. bin/rtlpaths.exe traces the same routes on the
Hardcaml graph, where every signal still has its name and origin.

Summary
- Every one of the 81 (4 PEs) and 265 (8 PEs) violating endpoints is a PE register (S, P, F, L, pv
  of upe_rtl.ml), and every worst path starts at the edge sampler's registered outputs
  (edge_sampler.ml:146, "valid" and "bit"; now named es_valid and es_bit_raw).
- Route (rtlpaths, confirmed by the STA cell counts of 35-38 logic cells): es_valid -> es_level
  (chip_rtl.ml, the held recovered bit) -> segment 0's fixed port -> PE 0's A input (the
  segment's source multiplexer, upe_rtl.ml:260) -> window subtract and bitmap read (115-118) ->
  g multiplexer (125) -> THE g CHAIN: every PE's g source 7 takes the previous PE's g, across
  segment boundaries, one multiplexer per PE (upe_rtl.ml:125 and the g wires, 239) -> the last PE's
  gating, negation and 17-bit add (134-139), overflow and saturation (142-145), result
  multiplexer (153), S/P/F/L next-state multiplexers (160-174) -> register.
  The step chain (follow, upe_rtl.ml:158) ripples through every PE the same way and feeds every
  PE register's enable. The g chain is why 8 PEs are 5 ns worse than 4: 7 hops instead of 3.
- Without the edge sampler's registers (4 PEs) the same PE route starts at the segment control register
  (upe_rtl.ml:228, the source select of segment 0): -1.38 ns (4 PEs), -3.98 ns (8 PEs).
- Second family: the programme store's SRAM output -> sequencer decode -> the threads'
  accumulator and pc registers (sequencer2.ml:81 and 83). The macro's clock-to-output is 6.25 ns
  at this corner (its liberty), leaving 10 ns at 60 MHz: +0.49 ns at 4 PEs but -4.67 ns at 8 PEs,
  where the placement is longer.
- Third: the input stage (stage.ml:63) -> the edge sampler's four unrolled sub-sample steps
  (edge_sampler.ml:134, 146): 31-36 cells, +2.3 ns (4 PEs) and -0.10 ns (8 PEs) at 20 ns.
- Fourth: the reset synchroniser (tt_top.ml:45, now reset_sync) -> the clear of every register:
  +1.07 ns at 4 PEs, 11 cells, mostly fan-out buffering.
- Most of the path delay is not logic depth but undersized drivers: on the worst 4-PE path,
  seven cells (buf_1 fan-out buffers, an and2_1, an a221oi_1, an a22oi_1, a nand2_1) each take
  0.86-1.41 ns driving 0.12-0.25 pF with 1.0-1.7 ns slews, about 7.5 ns of its 24.8 ns. The
  flow's setup repair ran on placement parasitics, reported "No setup violations found" and did
  not size for the routed wires; no maximum transition was set (MAX_TRANSITION_CONSTRAINT None).
