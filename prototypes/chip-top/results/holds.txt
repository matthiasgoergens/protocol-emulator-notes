Hold buffers: where they were and why (synth/holds.py; run directories as named).

Cause. LibreLane's base.sdc applies the 0.25 ns clock uncertainty to hold as well as setup, and
the flow adds 0.1 ns of hold margin after placement. sg13cmos5l has no enable flip-flop, so every
register with an enable or a synchronous clear is a flip-flop whose Q returns to its own D through
one or two gates. Such a self-loop has no skew and no jitter between launch and capture (same
flip-flop, same edge), but its clock-to-Q plus one gate is shorter than 0.35 ns plus the hold
time at the typical corner, so the flow buffered almost all of them. The both-edges pin stage and
the macros were not the cause: 102 of the 3,206 buffers (3 %) sit behind the stage's flip-flops,
none behind a macro output, and under 20 feed macro pins (the worst hold endpoint before repair
was the 512x16's A_DIN, but it took few buffers).

Measured on the CTS database of the 4-PE harden (results/harden-pe4), typical corner, endpoints
with hold slack under the 0.1 ns margin, by hold uncertainty: 0.25 ns 1,692; 0.15 ns 72; 0.10 ns
27; 0.05 ns 16; 0 ns 9. Fix: tt/src/chip.sdc, base.sdc with the uncertainty on setup only and
0.1 ns on hold (propagated clocks, the 5 % derate and the flow's hold margins stay).
