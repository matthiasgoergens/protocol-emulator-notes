8. **The yield figures are conditional arithmetic, not validated yield predictions. — OK as labelled assumptions; UNSUPPORTED as process estimates.** `yield.py` explicitly assumes Gaussian overlay, a recalled 45 nm 3σ figure, and a 0.03 µm clearance ([yield.py](/home/matthias/prog/janestreet/prog/janestreet/emulator-wt-rule-breaks/prototypes/rule-breaks/yield/yield.py:5)). Clean DRC summaries therefore support only the tested geometry under the specified decks; they do not cover LVS, edge/periphery integration, fabrication qualification, or all possible strap/edge interactions.

**Overall verdict:** the main procedural distinction, SRAM-marker handling, upstream `Cnt.c.SRAM` rule, area calculations for the listed generated geometries, and nominal SPICE failure examples are substantially supported. The README contains at least one factual error (the 204-vs-56 DRC count), presents unverified generator equivalence and relaxed-deck substitutions too confidently, and bases its yield and process-physics conclusions on explicit assumptions. Its recommendation is a useful hypothesis for further review, not a demonstrated tape-in or silicon-ready result.
