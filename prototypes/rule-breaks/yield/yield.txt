   NW 0.24 (well edge, overlay part only)         0.120  1.24e-15  1.97e-09
   Cnt.c 0.02 fails at almost any overlay: the contact then lands partly on STI. IHP's own
   bit cell does exactly this (0.02, upstream deck allows 0.006 under the SRAM marker), so the
   process must tolerate a contact straddling the Activ edge; the cost is contact resistance
   and junction leakage at that contact, not a hard failure. Keep it off storage nodes.
