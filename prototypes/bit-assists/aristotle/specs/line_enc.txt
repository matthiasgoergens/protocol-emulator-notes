Module line_enc: a line encoder with three modes, selected by cfg_mode[1:0]: 0 = pass, 1 = NRZI,
2 = Manchester (IEEE 802.3 convention); 3 behaves as pass. cfg_invert inverts every symbol;
cfg_toggle_on selects the NRZI bit value that toggles the level (USB toggles on 0).

Inputs: tick (emit one symbol this clock), bit (the data bit offered), restart, cfg_mode,
cfg_invert, cfg_toggle_on. Outputs: need_bit, sym, line.

need_bit (combinational): whether `bit` is consumed at this tick. Always 1 in pass and NRZI
mode. In Manchester mode a bit takes two ticks (two half-bit symbols) and need_bit is 1 only at
the first half of a bit cell.

sym (combinational): the symbol this tick emits, before cfg_invert is applied:
- pass: sym = bit;
- NRZI: a level register; the new level = level XOR (bit == cfg_toggle_on), and sym is the new
  level; the level register takes the new level at a tick in NRZI mode;
- Manchester: at the first half of a bit cell sym is the complement of `bit` and the bit is
  latched; at the second half sym is the latched bit. So a 1 is sent low then high.
Then sym is XORed with cfg_invert, in every mode.

line: a register that takes sym at every clock with tick = 1 and holds otherwise; line shows
each symbol from the clock after its tick.

restart = 1 at a clock edge puts NRZI at the idle level 0 and Manchester at the start of a bit
cell (and clears the latched bit), taking precedence over a tick in the same clock. It does not
affect line.

Consequence to check: in Manchester mode, the two symbols of every bit cell differ, and the
second equals the data bit XOR cfg_invert.
